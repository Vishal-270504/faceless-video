You type your password and click login. How does the server know it's really you?

That's authentication. It answers one question: who are you?

Passwords never get stored as plain text. The server stores a hash, a scrambled version that can't be turned back into your password.

Right password? The server sends back a token, a signed ticket with your identity on it.

Your app sends that token with every request. If it's missing or invalid, the server replies 401 Unauthorized. In plain words: I don't know who you are.

The takeaway: authentication proves who you are. Hashing keeps your password safe.
