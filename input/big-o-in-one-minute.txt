Two solutions both work on your laptop. One breaks with a million users. Big O explains why.

Big O describes how the work grows as the input grows. It is about the trend, not the speed of one run.

A loop over n items is O of n: double the items, and the work doubles.

A loop inside a loop, over every pair, is O of n squared: double the items, and the work quadruples.

Looking up a key in a hash map is usually O of 1: the size barely matters.

Takeaway: ask what happens when the list is a hundred times bigger.
