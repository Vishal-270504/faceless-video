Your test adds a wait of two seconds, and still fails sometimes. Here is why.

A fixed wait guesses how long the page takes. On a slow machine, the guess is wrong. On a fast one, you wait for nothing.

Playwright auto waits. Before a click, it checks the element is visible, enabled and stable, so it never clicks too early.

For checks, expect the text Saved to be visible. It retries until it passes or times out.

A timeout is a limit, not a delay. A fast page passes right away.

Takeaway: wait for the thing you need, not for time to pass.
