You can climb one or two steps at a time. How many different ways reach the top of ten steps?

One step has one way. Two steps have two ways.

The rule: the ways to reach step n equal the ways to reach step n minus one, plus the ways to reach step n minus two. Fill a table from the bottom, one step at a time.

Each step needs only the two before it, so keep just the last two numbers.

Plain recursion repeats the same calls. The loop makes one pass.

For ten steps the answer is 89.

Takeaway: find the rule first, then fill the table.
