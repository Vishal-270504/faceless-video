Two apps can do the same job. One feels cheap. The other feels professional. The difference is small details.

Errors: don't say "Something went wrong." Show the problem under the field that's wrong, and clear it when it's fixed.

Delete: ask first. Cancel is focused by default, and Escape closes the dialog. Only the red button deletes.

Feedback: after saving, show a short toast. "Note created." It disappears after three seconds.

Helper text: tell people the rules before they break them. "At least 3 characters."

Access: use visible labels, a clear focus ring, and real buttons. Keyboard users depend on them.

Feedback and clarity are cheap. They change how the whole app feels.
