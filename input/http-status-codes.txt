Your app shows a blank error, and the only clue is a number like 404. Here is what the numbers mean.

HTTP status codes tell the client how a request went. They come in five families.

1xx: information.

2xx: success. 200 means OK, 201 means created.

3xx: redirect. Go elsewhere to finish.

4xx: your request is wrong. 400 is bad input, 401 means log in, 403 means logged in but not allowed, 404 means not found.

5xx: the server failed. 500 means something broke on the server.

Takeaway: the first digit tells you who to check. Four means the request, five means the server.
