Big questions get weak answers. When you ask for everything at once, the AI has to guess.

Two habits fix it. Give context first, then ask for a plan before the answer.

Context means what the project is, what you tried, and what you need. A few sentences are enough.

Example: instead of build me a shop, say: I have a React app with a cart. Give me a five-step plan, and wait for my OK before any code.

Then break big asks into small ones. Login first, then cart, then checkout.

Takeaway: context, then a plan, then one small step at a time.
