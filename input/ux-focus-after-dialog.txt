You open a dialog, close it, and your cursor jumps to the top of the page. Now you have to find your place again. That's a focus problem.

Focus is the element that receives the next key press. Keyboard users and screen readers depend on it.

Broken version: a dialog opens, but focus stays behind it. Press Tab, and you're moving through the page behind the dialog.

The fix: when a dialog opens, move focus inside it. Keep Tab inside until the dialog closes.

When it closes, send focus back to the button that opened it. The user continues exactly where they were.

Press Escape to close. And give focus a visible outline, so users can see where they are.

The takeaway: a dialog opens with focus inside, and closes with focus back where it started.
