Your main button sits in the top right corner. Now try to tap it with one hand on a big phone. Your thumb can't reach it.

Most people hold their phone in one hand. The thumb reaches the bottom of the screen easily. The top corners are hard.

Broken version: the Save button sits under the status bar, far from the thumb.

The fix: move the main action to the bottom, in a bar that stays in place while you scroll.

Keep less important actions up top, like back or settings. Those can be a stretch, because you use them less.

The takeaway: put the action you use most where the thumb already rests.
