UI and UX
are two words
people mix up.
They are related,
but they are
not the same
job.
UI means user
interface.
It is what
you see and
touch,
the buttons, the
colors, the fonts,
and the layout.
UX means user
experience.
It is how
the whole thing
feels to use,
whether you can
finish your task,
how long it
takes, and whether
you feel stuck.
Here is a
simple way to
remember it.
UI is the
look,
UX is the
path.
Imagine a checkout
page.
The UI decides
that the pay
button is blue,
big, and easy
to see.
The UX decides
that you can
pay in three
taps that the
form remembers your
address,
and that you
get a clear
message when the
payment fails.
A page can
look beautiful and
still feel bad
to use.
A page can
also work well
but look messy.
Good products need
both.
The takeaway,
UI makes the
screen look right.
UX makes the
whole journey work.
Designers, developers,
and product people
all care about
both.
Which one do
you think matters
more?
Tell me in
the comments.
